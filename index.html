🌌 SpaceWise Academy is Landing in Dubai

Explore. Experiment. Build. Engineer. Discover.

This winter break, give your child an immersive, five-day STEM programme developed and delivered in partnership with EverCurious Space.

Think Like a Scientist. Create Like an Engineer. Communicate Like a Storyteller.

Delivered by genuine space veterans including television space journalist Kate Arkless Gray (Sky News / BBC) and 30-year Space Camp Vice President Michael Flachbart, SpaceWise Academy connects real-world space technology with hands-on challenges.

From designing lunar habitats to tasting real space food and launching custom model rockets, students work in collaborative 'crews' to solve authentic engineering problems facing NASA, the European Space Agency (ESA), and the UAE Space Agency.

✨ Hands-On Space STEM Curriculum

Six Core Space Missions

Action-packed engineering challenges, scientific experiments, and collaborative missions designed to ignite lifelong curiosity.

🚀

Go For Launch! Model Rocketry

Learn the physics of rocket propulsion, solid vs. liquid engines, and thrust. Each student builds an individual model rocket with engine mount, payload bay, and parachute recovery to keep!

Rocketry & Physics
🥚

Save the Eggstronauts!

Explore spacecraft design, atmospheric re-entry friction, and heat shielding. In crews, design and construct a capsule that survives a real drop test to keep your crew alive!

Aerospace Engineering
🛰️

Astronaut Life & Space Food

Discover how astronauts live, sleep, and conduct spacewalks on the ISS. Learn how food is prepared for zero-gravity and sample authentic NASA & Russian freeze-dried space food and ice-cream!

Human Spaceflight
🌕

Moon Race 2.0 & Lunar Base

Explore the global race back to the lunar surface with Artemis and commercial space pioneers. Design and engineer a sustainable lunar base equipped with power, life support, and research labs.

Planetary Science
🔴

Missions to Mars Design

Grasp the immense scale of the solar system and the challenges of deep space travel. Teams engineer a complete architecture for humanity's first crewed journey to the Red Planet.

Mission Architecture
🇦🇪

UAE in Space & Commercial Sector

Learn about the groundbreaking achievements of the UAE Space Agency and MBRSC (Hope Probe, Rashid Rover, astronaut missions) and discover exciting career pathways in the expanding space economy.

Space Economy & Careers

Space Camp Operations VP

🗓️ Mission Roadmap

The 5-Day Flight Plan

Each day runs from 9:00 AM to 3:30 PM with morning and lunch breaks. Click each mission day to explore focus areas.

🚀 Day 1 — Monday, 14 December

Welcome, Why Space? & Capsule Design

Launch into space history! Form mission crews, explore how rockets escape Earth's gravity, and begin engineering design planning for the Space Capsule Challenge.

Mission Focus Areas

  • Camp orientation, safety briefing & 'Mission Manual' distribution
  • Why Space? Breakthroughs and humanity's future beyond Earth
  • Crew assignments & team identity formulation
  • Brief history of spaceflight & getting to and from orbit
  • Space Capsule Engineering Challenge: Planning session in small groups
  • The Language of Space & aerospace terminology
🛰️ Day 2 — Tuesday, 15 December

Living on the ISS & Rocket Construction I

Step aboard the International Space Station! Experience interactive microgravity demonstrations, plan your crew mission patch, and commence building your model rocket.

Mission Focus Areas

  • Living and working on the ISS with interactive student demonstrations
  • Mission Patch history, symbolism & planning in small crews
  • Rocket Revolution: Rocket propulsion physics & engine mechanics
  • Rocket Construction: Build Session I (body tubes, engine mounts, fins)
  • Space Capsule Challenge: Hands-on fabrication & heat shield testing
🔴 Day 3 — Wednesday, 16 December

Missions to Mars & Space Food Tasting

Look towards the Red Planet! Tackle the Mars Mission Architecture challenge, finalize mission patches, continue rocket builds, and dine like an astronaut tasting freeze-dried food!

Mission Focus Areas

  • Missions to Mars: Distance, scale of inner solar system & interplanetary radiation
  • Mars Mission Design Engineering Challenge: Group architecture session
  • Mission Patch design refinement & detailing
  • Rocket Construction: Build Session II (payload bay & recovery deployment)
  • Dine Like an Astronaut: Taste authentic NASA & Russian freeze-dried space food and ice-cream!
  • Space Capsule Challenge: Final build & safety checks
🎨 Day 4 — Thursday, 17 December

Rocket Painting & The Eggstronaut Drop Test

Final rocket assembly and custom livery painting! Refine Mars mission concepts, and subject your crew capsules to the ultimate drop test to see which eggstronauts survive!

Mission Focus Areas

  • Rocket Building Session III: Parachute rigging & aerodynamic balance checks
  • Rocket Painting & Custom Livery: Spray painting & detailing your craft
  • Mars Mission Engineering Challenge: Presentation preparation & design review
  • The Great Capsule Drop Test: Testing space capsules under gravity to determine eggstronaut survival!
  • Evaluation, data collection & engineering redesign analysis
🎖️ Day 5 — Friday, 18 December

Presentations & Graduation Ceremony

The grand finale! Present Mars architectures, explore the UAE's role in space, and celebrate graduation with parents, receiving Trainee Pins and Space-Flown Metal Wings!

Mission Focus Areas

  • Postcards to Space design activity
  • Complete Crew Mission Patches showcase
  • International Space Programmes & the UAE in Space (UAE Space Agency & MBRSC)
  • Mars Mission Design: Student crew team presentations
  • Formal Graduation Ceremony: Parents and families warmly invited!
  • Presentation of Certificates, SpaceWise Trainee Pins, and Space-Flown Metal Wings
  • Pack up custom rockets & mission manuals to take home!
🎓 Lifelong STEM Skills

What Your Child Will Gain

SpaceWise Academy develops the analytical mindset, collaborative spirit, and communication skills needed to excel in science, technology, and beyond.

  • ✓ Rocketry & Applied Physics — Understand rocket motors, propulsion, trajectory, and recovery systems through direct building.
  • ✓ Engineering Design Thinking — Iterate, test under pressure, and redesign space capsules against real physical constraints.
  • ✓ Teamwork & Crew Leadership — Assume mission roles, manage budgets, negotiate compromises, and solve open-ended challenges.
  • ✓ Confident Storytelling & Presentation — Pitch Mars mission architectures clearly and persuasively before an audience.
  • ✓ Global & UAE Space Literacy — Connect with the real aerospace industry (NASA, ESA, UAE Space Agency, and MBRSC).
