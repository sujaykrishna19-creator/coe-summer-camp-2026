🗓️ Daily Programme

The 5-Day Journey

Each day builds on the last — from first discoveries to the grand exhibition. Click each day to see the detailed focus areas.

🚀 Day 1 — Monday, 13 July

CubeSat & Drone Flying

Blast off into the world of space tech and drones. Students begin their CubeSat mission planning and take their first drone flights.

Focus Areas

  • Camp orientation and safety briefing
  • Introduction to CubeSat and space technology
  • CubeSat mission planning and basic design
  • Introduction to drone components and controls
  • Basic drone flying practice
🔧 Day 2 — Tuesday, 14 July

CubeSat & Magician Go

Develop CubeSat models and meet the Magician Go robot. Program movements, navigate paths, and transport objects with robotics.

Focus Areas

  • CubeSat model development
  • Payload and design improvement
  • Introduction to Magician Go robotics
  • Robot movement and navigation
  • Object transportation simulation
🎯 Day 3 — Wednesday, 15 July

Drone Flying & Magician Go

Take on the obstacle avoidance challenge! Pilot drones through courses, program robot missions, and push your engineering skills.

Focus Areas

  • Drone flying skill development
  • Obstacle avoidance challenge
  • Magician Go programming and control
  • Robotics path planning
  • Testing, troubleshooting, and improvement
✈️ Day 4 — Thursday, 16 July

CubeSat & Drone Flying

Finalise CubeSat builds, complete drone challenges, experience flight simulation, and prepare projects for the grand exhibition.

Focus Areas

  • CubeSat final build and refinement
  • Drone flying challenge completion
  • Flight simulation and aerospace connection
  • Project display preparation
  • Presentation planning and rehearsal
🎉 Day 5 — Friday, 17 July

Exhibition & Celebration

The big day! Students showcase their satellites, robots, drones, and rockets to parents and guests. Presentations, demos, and certificates.

Focus Areas

  • CubeSat project display
  • Magician Go robotics demonstration
  • Drone flying and obstacle challenge showcase
  • Straw rocket and flight simulation display, where applicable
  • Student team presentations
  • Certificates and recognition ceremony
